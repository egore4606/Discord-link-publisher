# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include empathy, respectful feedback, accepting responsibility, and focusing on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, sexualized attention, publishing another person's private information, and other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that are inconsistent with this Code of Conduct. Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. All complaints will be reviewed fairly and confidentially.
